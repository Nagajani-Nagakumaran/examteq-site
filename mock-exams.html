Year 5 11+ Paper Class

A structured, 2-hour class covering Maths, English and Creative Writing. Places are limited—book early to secure a seat.

Year 5 (11+ prep) 2 hours per class Maths • English • Creative Writing Small groups • In-centre
Availability: Saturday, Monday, Wednesday, and Friday 5–7pm. If a class is full, you can join the waiting list below.

What’s Included (Every Class)

  • Maths: Weekly numeracy assessment + targeted teaching.
  • English: Comprehension, grammar, spelling, vocabulary, punctuation, cloze.
  • Creative Writing: Technique mini-lesson + in-class essay writing.
  • Feedback: Individual pointers on strengths and next steps.

Answers to the harder Maths questions are explained in an online video on our YouTube channel.

Maths Testing Format

Stage 1 — Foundation Decimal Topic Test (MCQ): warm-up multiple choice to consolidate fundamentals.

Stage 2 — Advanced Topic Test (Standard format): written solutions required; workings and reasoning emphasised.

Weeks 1–2: Decimals • Weeks 3–4: Fractions

4-Week Teaching Plan

Clear goals each week to build mastery and exam confidence.

Week 1

Maths: Decimals English: Core skills Writing: Diary Entry

Stage 1 MCQ + Stage 2 written. Writing focus: voice, tense control, structure.

Week 2

Maths: Decimals English: Comprehension Writing: Diary Entry

Fluency and problem solving with decimals; deeper inference in comprehension.

Week 3

Maths: Fractions English: Grammar & Cloze Writing: Informal Letter

Methods for equivalence, addition/subtraction; accurate sentence craft.

Week 4

Maths: Fractions English: Spelling & Vocabulary Writing: Informal Letter

Mixed fraction problems; precise word choice and cohesive letter structure.
